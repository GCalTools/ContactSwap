Older versions and release notes are on the [Releases](https://github.com/GCalTools/ContactSwap/releases) page.

## What it does

- Copy contacts from Google to Outlook, from Outlook to Google, or between two accounts
- Contact photos come across too, at their original quality
- Back up your contacts to a .zip file and restore them to any account later
- Spot duplicates by email, phone or name, and choose to skip, replace, merge or keep both
- Labels, categories and Outlook folders carry across
- Undo replaced and merged contacts, and resume a copy that was interrupted
